CVPR paper template

The CVPR template in LOGICA (templates/cvpr) is based on the official
CVPR/ICCV/3DV LaTeX author kit:

  https://github.com/cvpr-org/author-kit

cvpr.sty, preamble.tex and the @String abbreviations in main.bib are copied
from the author kit unchanged. ieeenat_fullname.bst is a modification of
abbrvnat.bst by Patrick W Daly, distributed under the LaTeX Project Public
License (see the header of that file). The author kit is maintained by the
CVPR organizers and contributors listed in its README.

main.tex and the files in sec/ follow the structure of the author kit with
short placeholder text in place of the author guidelines.
