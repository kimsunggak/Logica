\begin{abstract}
Video-based human pose estimation degrades sharply when body parts are occluded.
We present \method{}, which estimates per-joint uncertainty and routes each joint
through spatial or temporal reasoning accordingly. On two benchmarks, \method{}
reduces the error on occluded joints while keeping the cost of inference low.
\end{abstract}
