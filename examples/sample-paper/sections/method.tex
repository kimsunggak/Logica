\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Overview}
\method{} has two stages: a per-frame pose network that also predicts
uncertainty, and a routing module that decides how much temporal context each
joint receives.

\subsection{Uncertainty Estimation}
\label{sec:uncertainty}
For each joint we predict a mean $\mu_{t,j}$ and a variance $\sigma^2_{t,j}$ and
train with the Gaussian negative log-likelihood
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}_{\text{nll}} = \sum_{t,j} \frac{\lVert \mathbf{p}_{t,j} - \mu_{t,j} \rVert^2}{2\sigma^2_{t,j}} + \log \sigma_{t,j}.
  \label{eq:nll}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Temporal Routing}
\label{sec:routing}
Joints with high variance in Eq.~\eqref{eq:nll} are routed to a temporal
transformer \citep{vaswani2017attention}; the others keep their per-frame
estimate. This keeps the cost low because most joints are visible most of the
time.
