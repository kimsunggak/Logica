\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{Motivation}
Human pose estimation from video is a building block for action recognition,
sports analytics and rehabilitation \citep{cao2017realtime}. Previous methods do
not consider uncertainty and therefore have problems when the image is occluded,
which is very common in real world videos where people overlap with each other
or with objects in the scene.

\subsection{Problem Statement}
Given a sequence of frames $\{I_t\}_{t=1}^{T}$, we estimate the 2D location
$\mathbf{p}_{t,j} \in \R^2$ of every joint $j$ in every frame $t$.
A joint may be visible in some frames and hidden in others.

\subsection{Contributions}
Our contributions are as follows.
\begin{itemize}
  \item We propose per-joint uncertainty estimation for video pose estimation.
  \item We introduce temporal routing that uses neighbouring frames only for
        uncertain joints (Section~\ref{sec:routing}).
  \item We show consistent gains on occluded joints (Table~\ref{tab:main}).
\end{itemize}
