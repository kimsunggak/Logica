\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Human Pose Estimation}
Image-based methods regress heatmaps with convolutional networks
\citep{cao2017realtime} or transformers \citep{vaswani2017attention}.

\subsection{Video-based Methods}
Video methods aggregate features over time, for example with temporal
convolutions \citep{pavllo2019videopose}, but they treat every joint the same way.
